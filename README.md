Battery pack. You will be connecting the output of your Type-C charger module (the BAT and GND pads on the right side of the board) to the main Input/Output pads on your BMS.Charger BAT ➔ connects to ➔ BMS P+ (Sometimes labeled C+ or Pack+)Charger GND ➔ connects to ➔ BMS P- (Sometimes labeled C- or Pack-)Crucial Rule: Never connect the charger directly to the battery terminals (B+ and B-). All charging power must flow through the BMS P+ and P- pads so the BMS can monitor the voltage and cut off the power when the batteries are full.1.Prepare the Charger Board:Locate the output pads.Look at the right side of your Type-C charger module (the side opposite the USB port). You will see two large solder holes labeled BAT and GND. Tin these pads with a little bit of solder.2.Prepare the BMS:Locate the main charge/discharge pads on your BMS. They are usually labeled P+ and P- (or sometimes C+/C-).3.Solder the Positive Wire:Solder a wire from the BAT pad on the charger to the P+ pad on the BMS.4.Solder the Negative Wire: Solder a wire from the GND pad on the charger to the P- pad on the BMS. You will connect the Input pads of your buck converter directly to the main P+ and P- pads on the BMS (the exact same pads you just soldered your Type-C charger to).Buck Converter IN+ ➔ connects to ➔ BMS P+Buck Converter IN- (or GND) ➔ connects to ➔ BMS P-1.Locate Buck Converter Inputs:Find the input side of your buck converter. It will have pads labeled IN+ and IN- (or sometimes VIN and GND).2.Wire the Positive Side:Solder a wire from the IN+ pad on the buck converter to the P+ pad on your BMS. (It is perfectly fine to solder this wire directly on top of the charger's positive wire that is already on the P+ pad).3. Wire the Negative Side: Solder a wire from the IN- pad on the buck converter to the P- pad on your BMS. (Again, share this pad with the charger's ground wire). How to Wire the SwitchYou will intercept the positive wire going to your buck converter. Do not put a switch on the negative/ground wire (it can cause weird grounding issues).1.Wire from BMS to Switch:Solder a wire from the P+ pad on your BMS to the first pin on your switch.2.Wire from Switch to Buck Converter:Solder a wire from the second pin of your switch to the IN+ pad on your buck converter.3.Leave the Negative Wire Alone:The negative wire should still go straight from the BMS P- directly to the Buck Converter IN- (sharing the pad with the charger's negative wire, just like before).Warning: Never place the switch between the batteries and the BMS. The BMS must always stay connected to the batteries to monitor them properly.
